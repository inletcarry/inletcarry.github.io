How to use your INLET

Push, fan out, grab. Here’s everything else in a minute.

01

Open it

Hold the wallet with the lever at the bottom. Push the lever with your thumb and your cards slide up and fan out, so you can see every one at a glance.

02

Close it

Press the fanned cards gently back down with your fingertip until they sit flush inside the case.

03

Load your cards

Slide cards into the top opening one by one, all facing the same way. Put the card you use most at the front so it pops up first.

04

Use the band

The elastic band on the back holds extra cards, a few folded bills or a receipt. Slip them under the band flat.

Fit tip

Fill the case until the cards slide in smoothly. If you have to force the last card, leave it out. Raised-number (embossed) cards take more room than flat ones, so mix them in. If the pop-up feels stiff or won’t fan out fully, take one card out and try again.

Care

  • Wipe the case with a soft, dry cloth. No harsh cleaners, and don’t soak it.
  • Don’t overstuff the elastic band or fold it backwards, so it keeps its stretch.
  • Keep it in a different pocket from your keys to protect the matte finish.
  • Don’t force the lever. If it sticks, remove a card and try again.

FAQ

How many cards does it hold?

Your everyday cards fit in the pop-up case, and the band carries extras. It depends on how thick your cards are, so go by the fit tip above: if a card won’t slide in easily, the case is full.

Does it block contactless scanning?

The case is made of aluminum, a material commonly used in RFID-blocking wallets to help shield cards stored inside. To tap and pay, pop the card out first. Cards under the elastic band sit outside the case.

My cards don’t pop up all the way.

The case is probably too full or holding a thick card. Remove one card and push the lever again.

Can I keep the INLET card inside?

Sure, or swap it for one of your own cards. Keep it somewhere handy since it brings you back to this guide.
